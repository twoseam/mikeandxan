FAQ

When is the RSVP deadline?

We ask that you RSVP by October 1st to help us finalize our wedding plans.

What time should I arrive at the ceremony?

Approximately 15–20 minutes prior to be seated and settled for the ceremony to begin on time.

Are kids welcome?

While we love your kids, we decided to have an adults-only celebration so parents can have a night out and party with us! The only kids in attendance will be those in the wedding party.

What's the dress code?

Think polished and stylish but not overly dressy. No need for evening gowns or tuxedos!

Ultimately, we want you to be comfortable, present, and enjoying yourself, so wear whatever will help YOU feel that way! We're treating this as more of a party than a wedding, but we'd still love to see everyone dressed up in some way.

Can I bring a date?

Our guest list is finalized to the names on each invitation. Please contact us directly if we missed your plus one and we'll get them on the list.

Is there parking for the event?

Parking is available at the event space. Please see the map below noting designated parking areas.
