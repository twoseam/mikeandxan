Our Story

After matching on a dating app back in 2017, we decided right then and there that we'd hang out forever & ever & ever. Our wedding day will be our 3,507th day together — almost a decade.

April 8, 2017

Our first date

We met on Bumble and texted nonstop for two weeks. When we finally met, drinks at Brewery Emperial and ramen at Komatsu. Michael saved Alexandria as "HOT SOUP."

May 1, 2018

Our first move

Michael left his job to start a business. We found a safer spot and moved into Midtown together — the first of many moves over the next three years.

July 1, 2021

Bought a home together

Having a home together was something we both always wanted. Supporting one another in our careers gave us the chance. We feel lucky to call this house "home."

December 24, 2025

The proposal

We left the movie early because Michael got too worked up. We pulled to the side of the road, and the proposal happened right then and there. The movie was fine, btw.

FAQ

When is the RSVP deadline?

We ask that you RSVP by October 1st to help us finalize our wedding plans.

What time should I arrive?

Approximately 15–20 minutes prior to be seated for the ceremony to begin on time.

Are kids welcome?

We decided on an adults-only celebration so parents can have a night out. The only kids will be those in the wedding party.

What's the dress code?

Polished and stylish but not overly dressy. No evening gowns or tuxedos needed — we're treating this as a party.

Can I bring a date?

Our guest list is finalized to the names on each invitation. Contact us if we missed your plus one.
